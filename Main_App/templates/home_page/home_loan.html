Documents Required for a Home Loan

Keep these ready to speed up approval. Your lender may ask for more.

All applicants

  • Identity proof (Aadhaar, passport or voter ID)
  • PAN card
  • Address proof
  • Passport size photographs
  • Last 6 months bank statements

Salaried

  • Latest salary slips
  • Form 16 or ITR for recent years
  • Employment or appointment letter
  • Salary account statement

Self-employed

  • ITR with computation for recent years
  • Profit and loss account and balance sheet
  • Business proof (GST, registration)
  • Business account statements

Property documents such as the sale agreement, title papers, approved plan and tax receipts are also required and are checked by the lender.

Tax Benefits on Home Loans

Under the old tax regime, you may be able to claim:

  • Principal repayment under Section 80C, within the overall limit
  • Interest on a self-occupied property under Section 24(b), within the limit
  • Additional deductions may apply in some cases

Tax rules change. Please confirm with a qualified tax advisor.
